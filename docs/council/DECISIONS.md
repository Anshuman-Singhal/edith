# E.D.I.T.H. Council — Decision Log

Applies to [PLAN.md v1](../PLAN.md). Date: 2026-11-23. Editor: plan editor (merge of round-1 proposals in `r1/`).

ID prefixes:
- `L*` are binding lead rulings.
- `C*` are council resolutions made by the editor. Where no ruling applied, these follow L11: the simplest option that proves the four goals.

Changing any entry needs a new entry that supersedes it. Do not edit entries in place.

## Council resolutions

| ID | Decision | Alternatives (who) | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| C01 | **pymavlink 2.4.50** implements the PX4 `AssetAdapter`. MAVSDK 4.x (`==4.2.1`) is allowed behind the same interface only if the team prefers it later. The PLAN gives MAVLink messages plus MAVSDK 3.x plugin names as reference. | MAVSDK-Python 4.2.0 with bundled `mavsdk_server` (architect). | Sim probed that MAVSDK ≥4.0 (2026-09-22) is a ctypes rewrite with no `mavsdk_server`, a new API and 9 releases in 8 weeks. pymavlink is stable, has cp39–cp314 wheels, and MockVehicle can share its codec. One library serves both sides. |
| C02 | The canonical verb enum stays `takeoff, goto, orbit, observe, search, follow, hold, rtl, land`. SME words are spoken synonyms: SCOUT→search, OVERWATCH→observe, RETURN/RTB→rtl, LAUNCH→takeoff. | SME's 6-verb operator vocabulary as the enum (defense_sme); voice's table. | Keeps the architect's structural allowlist and the MAVLink mapping one-to-one, while operators still use their own words. |
| C03 | A bare `WATCH` while an alert is shown is an alert response: it pins the contact and inset and **moves nothing**. `<callsign> watch <target>` is an `observe` proposal that needs confirmation. | SME's "TRACK keeps it under observation" (ambiguous about whether the drone moves). | Anything that moves an asset must go through confirmation (L1). Disambiguating by the presence of a callsign is trivial for the parser and safe. |
| C04 | Confirmed machine tracks are **published to TAK by default** as `a-u-*` with remarks "machine · unconfirmed". MARK republishes with `how=h-e`. Config `tak.publish_machine_tracks` can restrict publication to marks only. | Only human MARKs go to the team (defense_sme §1.1); publish detections as unknown (architect). | L3 says machine detections are "published as unknown". Publishing proves TAK integration and the team picture with no extra operator step. The switch covers the SME's flooding concern once the interviews weigh in. |
| C05 | Machine detections are drawn as a **yellow open quatrefoil** (2525 unknown frame) with a P/V letter. A human mark is a filled quatrefoil. A human-reclassified hostile is a red open diamond. | Amber open diamond (hud.md, wireframe v0). | A diamond means hostile in 2525 (defense_sme), which contradicts L3. Shape carries the meaning per hud.md's monochrome-first rule. |
| C06 | The confirmation window is **10 s**, with a 600 ms lockout (extended until TTS ends). It never auto-confirms. | 6 s (defense_sme). | Architect, HUD and voice all chose 10 s, and the card countdown is designed around it. 6 s is tight for a deliberate second PTT press under load. The study measures confirmation fatigue. |
| C07 | Confirm methods: voice "CONFIRM" alone (second press), **temple two-finger hold ≥1 s**, or **companion `/ui/touch` hold ≥1 s**. The temple method is enabled only if bring-up test T7 shows 0 false triggers. | Voice + nonce only, no gesture confirm (platform); companion touch only (voice). | Quiet mode and the PM's "silent tasking" demo need a non-voice confirm. A two-finger 1 s hold with a progress ring is deliberate (hud.md). Gating on T7 covers platform's sensitivity concern. |
| C08 | The STOP gesture is a **temple triple-tap → hold all airborne assets**. The companion web UI also has a HOLD ALL button. | No gesture specified (L1 requires one). | `TempleAction.TripleClick` exists in the SDK and is otherwise unused. A false triple-tap only causes a hold, which is safe, and it is logged. |
| C09 | If the target asset is landed, the proposal **composes takeoff + verb** under one read-back ("LAUNCH + SEARCH NAI 1") and one confirm. | Reject with "take off first". | The SME vignette tasks drones from STANDBY. One explicit confirm still covers every step, and the read-back names the launch. |
| C10 | Ranking: the severity tier wins outright. Within a tier the score is hud.md's additive formula, extended with ML's class weight and AOI bonus. Only **confirmed tracks** raise alerts (ML persistence gate). DISMISS suppresses the track plus a 15 m same-class disc for **10 min** (ML), overridden by tier escalation or ≥30% range closure (hud). | hud.md formula with 60 s suppression; ml.md multiplicative 0–100 priority. | The additive score is explainable and its signals are logged. A 60 s suppression would re-alert a dismissed false positive (e.g. the deer) every minute. The overrides keep it safe. |
| C11 | Pre-emption hysteresis: ≥0.2 score margin and ≥3 s dwell (hud). | ≥15 points and ≥4 s hold (ml). | The HUD owner owns cognitive-load policy, and the two proposals are functionally equivalent. One rule avoids ambiguity. |
| C12 | Link-loss doctrine: `NAV_DLL_ACT` = Return, `COM_DL_LOSS_T` = 20 s, per scenario. The HUD shows an "RTL in 0:12" countdown. MockVehicle mirrors this. | RTL (architect); hold then RTL (defense_sme); continue the mission via `COM_DLL_EXCEPT` (sim option). | The autopilot, not our software, defines the behavior. RTL is the standard safe default and is visible in the demo. `COM_DLL_EXCEPT` bit values are UNVERIFIED. Revisit with operator interviews. |
| C13 | Sim site: rural Piatt County, IL (40.0700 N, 88.6300 W; MGRS 16TCK), NAIP public-domain imagery, with fictional named points (NAI 1/2, CP 3). | Fictional terrain from DEM+OSM (defense_sme); the Philadelphia example (18S). | It is flat (the flat-earth homography holds), public domain, not sensitive, and already probed. It is also not near PHL airspace or any facility. |
| C14 | The SBS compositor is the primary RayNeo path: two 640×480 slots from one renderer, decoded video drawn twice, with no `BaseMirrorActivity` or `MirroringView`. | Mirrored single canvas, SBS as fallback (hud). | Application of L5.
